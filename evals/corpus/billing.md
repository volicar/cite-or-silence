# Billing

## Plans

The Free plan includes 10,000 requests per month. The Pro plan costs 49 dollars per month and includes 1 million requests. The Enterprise plan has custom pricing and a dedicated support engineer.

## Invoices

Invoices are generated on the first day of each month and sent by email to the billing contact. You can download past invoices as PDF files from the Billing page.

## Refunds

Annual subscriptions can be refunded within 14 days of purchase. Monthly subscriptions are not refundable, but you can cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the period.
